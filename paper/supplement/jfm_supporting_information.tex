\noindent\textbf{Scope.} This is a separate, anonymized supplement to the
Journal of Futures Markets reviewer manuscript. All market price measures
are USD per barrel, and Barchart end-of-day \texttt{Latest} values are
settlement-price proxies rather than independently verified trades or final
CME settlement marks. The bounded correlation study uses synthetic model
values only and is not a historical WTI market-calibration experiment.

\section{Liquidity, timing and data provenance}
The 2,381-row pricing panel includes 2,319 observations with zero recorded
trading volume. Only 62 observations on 13 valuation dates have positive
reported volume. The inferred ranking persists in that restriction, but
the sample is too small for broad claims about executable trading prices.
Valuation-date cluster resampling accounts for cross-contract dependence
within a date but not all serial dependence across nearby dates.

\section{Bounded numerical reference and full grid-sensitivity sweep}
The separate, pilot-informed synthetic check reuses the 160 independent
historical return datasets and 480 option-state rows per futures-correlation
weight, with three strikes per dataset. The inference and PI/PM prices
assume correlation $\rho=1$ throughout. The known-volatility arithmetic
payoff reference is calculated independently using randomized scrambled
Sobol with an analytical geometric-average control variate, at
$\rho\in\{1,0.75,0.5\}$. These are hypothetical correlations, not empirical
estimates for the WTI futures curve.

The original 513-point posterior grid was compared against grids of
1,025, 2,049, and 4,097 points, replaying the \emph{same}
historical sufficient statistics and QMC reference prices. Coverage
classifications are unchanged across all four grids; one reference
near an interval boundary at $\rho=0.75$ intersects its QMC 95\%
uncertainty range at the finer grids. The absolute posterior
quantile endpoints nevertheless change by up to 0.00523 USD/bbl
between 513 and 4,097 points. Hence classification stability
does not establish sub-0.001 USD/bbl endpoint accuracy.

\section{Robustness and interpretation}\label{sec:short_robustness}

Alternative physical-return assumptions do not materially change the comparison. A standardized Student-\(t\) model leaves the PI--PM difference small relative to historical-volatility pricing errors. Reconstructing first-nearby CL returns contract by contract gives a similar result: on the 2,381 out-of-sample observations, historical PI has MAE 2.6468 and RMSE 3.4451, compared with 2.6187 and 3.4090 using the continuous/front-month return proxy. Further historical-volatility benchmarks appear in Online Supplement Section S1.

The small empirical PI--PM difference is not attributable to numerical noise. High-precision Monte Carlo, independently scrambled Sobol simulation, and Curran conditioning agree on the difference to a precision well below its observed magnitude. Simulation-based calibration provides a separate check on the posterior implementation. The supplement also reports a posterior-RMS plug-in sensitivity (Section S2), alternative mappings from LO-implied volatilities to one APO volatility, and Cox--Ross--Rubinstein tree-refinement checks (Section S4). These numerical checks do not establish an exact arbitrage-free representation of the volatility surface.

The market evidence has important limits. Many APO settlements have zero reported trading volume, especially at long maturities. The historical-versus-implied ranking remains under positive-volume and open-interest restrictions, but some expiry-level samples are small (Online Supplement Section S3). Moreover, the main pricing comparison contains only 15 target dates and the external validation only 10. Valuation-date cluster resampling preserves dependence among contracts observed together, but cannot eliminate serial dependence across adjacent dates.

Finally, the one-factor constant-volatility model excludes richer futures-curve dynamics, stochastic volatility, jumps, and convenience yields. Its implied volatilities are model-equivalent pricing inputs, not uniquely identified risk-neutral diffusion parameters or direct estimates of a variance risk premium. The out-of-sample results establish a difference in pricing performance for the tested inputs and model, not a structural physical-versus-risk-neutral volatility decomposition.

The newer Databento check adds dates but does not remove its
measurement limitations. Forward averages and effective discount
factors are inferred from other \emph{same-date} option strikes;
the historical volatility proxy is estimated from earlier
parity-implied forward-average changes, not first-nearby CL
realized returns; and the lognormal terminal-average approximation
does not price the actual partly fixed Asian path. Original Barchart
end-of-day \texttt{Latest} fields are settlement proxies rather
than independently certified final exchange transaction prices.
CME Clearing switched AAO end-of-day settlement valuations to
its Curran model effective May 18, 2020
\citep{cmeclearing2020curran}. Thus, agreement
with end-of-day marks can partly capture persistence or replication
of exchange model values, rather than new information about
tradeable option prices.

A very small price correction need not have economic value.
For instance, the older October-expiry mean absolute PI--PM
difference is about \$0.000745/bbl, well below the standard
\$0.01/bbl quotation increment. For the extended conditional
holdout, the tiny differences between the market-IV Bayesian
models and unchanged lagged IV are not robustly distinct in the
paired monthly resampling analysis. In addition, early-2026
settlement-mark residual-interval undercoverage warns against
treating a well-fitted retrospective uncertainty distribution as
a stable predictive probability model across regimes.

The older Barchart/LO evaluation and the newer Databento
benchmark have different economic observables, providers,
pricing engines, maturity support and information sets. They
are \emph{separate analyses}, not pooled observations for one
common estimator. None directly validates executable hedge
profits or the implied-volatility risk premium. At the time
of this revision, direct vendor vintage/timestamp verification,
Barchart redistribution rights, underlying quote/trade
validation, full 2,366-row same-contract IV reconciliation and
independent end-to-end historical input replay remain open;
publication-level conclusions must remain conditional on
those checks.

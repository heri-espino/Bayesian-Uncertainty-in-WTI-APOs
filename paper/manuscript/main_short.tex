\abstract[Abstract]{
We examine the effect of historical-volatility parameter uncertainty on WTI Average Price Option (APO) values and compare historical with option-implied volatility inputs. Posterior integration (PI) averages conditional option values over the volatility posterior; posterior-mean plug-in pricing (PM) instead evaluates the model at posterior mean volatility. A second-order approximation links their difference to posterior variance and price curvature. Across 175,000 synthetic return histories, PI and PM can diverge when estimation samples are short and price curvature is high. In the October-2026 WTI sample, however, the mean absolute PI--PM difference is only \$0.000745 per barrel, despite a mean width of \$0.2733 per barrel for the central 95\% posterior interval of model prices. Posterior price dispersion and the PI--PM difference are thus distinct measures. In 2,381 out-of-sample APO observations, implied volatility estimated from earlier option dates gives substantially lower pricing errors than the historical-volatility benchmarks tested, including rolling windows, EWMA, and horizon-matched GARCH. The advantage persists with same-contract lagged implied volatility and with implied volatility from standard monthly WTI futures options. These results are conditional on the maintained one-factor pricing model.
}

\bmsection*{Data availability}
The WTI APO and CL histories used in the empirical application were obtained through a commercial Barchart subscription. The independent vanilla-WTI validation uses CME/NYMEX LO option and CL futures statistics accessed through Databento. Raw proprietary market-data files are not redistributed. The public repository provides code, query metadata, schemas, provenance records, diagnostics, hashes, and derived results consistent with the applicable data licenses.

\bmsection*{Code availability}
The research code is organized as the Python package \texttt{bayesian\_asian\_options}, with tests, deterministic experiment entry points, and committed derived results. The public repository is \url{https://github.com/heri-espino/Bayesian-Uncertainty-in-WTI-APOs}.

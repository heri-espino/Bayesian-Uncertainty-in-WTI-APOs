\abstract[Abstract]{
This paper studies when Bayesian uncertainty about historically estimated volatility materially changes the value of WTI Average Price Options. Statistical learning is conducted under the physical measure and derivative valuation under the risk-neutral measure, so physical drift uncertainty is not treated as pricing uncertainty. A large synthetic mechanism map uses 175,000 return histories across sample size, volatility, moneyness, maturity, and partial-fixing states. Posterior-integrated and posterior-mean plug-in prices are usually close, but the largest observed absolute gap reaches 0.1384 when historical information is scarce and the pricing map is highly curved; the second-order term $\tfrac12 C^{Q\prime\prime}(\bar\sigma)\operatorname{Var}(\sigma\mid\mathcal D)$ closely tracks this behavior. In the contract-consistent October-2026 WTI panel, posterior-integrated and posterior-mean RMSE are 0.5177 and 0.5183, respectively, and 0.1864 and 0.1869 among the six positive-volume contract-date observations. Independent multi-million-path pseudo-Monte Carlo and randomized Sobol benchmarks confirm that the sub-cent integration gaps are numerically resolved. The larger empirical effect comes from volatility specification: the median historical posterior mean is 0.4155, while the median full-cross-section APO-implied effective risk-neutral volatility is 0.4577. Using only APO implied-volatility observations from earlier dates reduces MAE from 0.3583 to 0.1297--0.1312 and RMSE from 0.4816 to 0.2312--0.2512 over 345 forward holdouts. Heavy-tailed physical-measure returns improve fit modestly but do not alter the hierarchy. Posterior integration is therefore a second-order refinement in the observed WTI application, while becoming economically relevant in weak-information, high-curvature regimes.
}

The option histories used in the empirical application were obtained through a commercial Barchart subscription. Redistribution of raw proprietary files is not assumed to be permitted. The public repository therefore prioritizes code, schemas, provenance records, diagnostics, hashes, and derived results consistent with the applicable data license.

\bmsection*{Code availability}
The research code is organized as an installable Python package, \texttt{bayesian\_asian\_options}, with documented APIs, tests, deterministic seeds, and reproducible experiment entry points. A versioned code snapshot will accompany the final article.

# Extended strict forward-in-time WTI APO validation

This report summarizes the production run under
`results/analysis/wti_extended_forward/`. The experiment uses only APO implied-volatility
observations from dates strictly earlier than each target valuation date.

## Coverage

Seven expiries have enough committed option and CL-curve data for the six-parameter
call/put smile:

- 2026-09: 198 holdouts over 8 target dates
- 2026-10: 280 holdouts over 10 target dates
- 2026-11: 285 holdouts over 11 target dates
- 2027-03: 339 holdouts over 13 target dates
- 2027-09: 429 holdouts over 13 target dates
- 2028-03: 445 holdouts over 15 target dates
- 2028-09: 405 holdouts over 15 target dates

Total: **2,381 holdout option-date observations**. June 2029 is excluded because its committed
cross-section never reaches the six contracts required by the smile specification.

## Pooled pricing result

| Model | Bias | MAE | RMSE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Historical-volatility PI baseline | 2.4030 | 2.6187 | 3.4090 |
| Expanding prior-date smile | -0.0697 | 0.1088 | 0.1725 |
| Previous-day smile | -0.0352 | 0.1075 | 0.1594 |

The expanding smile reduces pooled MAE by **95.8%** and RMSE by **94.9%** relative to the
historical-volatility PI baseline. The previous-day smile reduces MAE by **95.9%** and RMSE by
**95.3%**.

The improvement occurs in every admitted expiry. It is modest but clear in the 2026 expiries
and becomes very large in 2027--2028 because the long-window historical volatility increasingly
exceeds the effective volatility embedded in the long-dated APO marks.

|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2026-09 | 0.4155 | 0.4478 | +0.0323 |
| 2026-10 | 0.4155 | 0.4572 | +0.0419 |
| 2026-11 | 0.4155 | 0.4444 | +0.0289 |
| 2027-03 | 0.4155 | 0.3609 | -0.0542 |
| 2027-09 | 0.4155 | 0.3001 | -0.1151 |
| 2028-03 | 0.4157 | 0.2647 | -0.1507 |
| 2028-09 | 0.4157 | 0.2416 | -0.1742 |

These are **effective/model-equivalent risk-neutral volatilities under the maintained one-factor
pricing map**. They must not be interpreted as estimates of a unique structural diffusion
coefficient. Risk premia, smile/skew, futures-curve dynamics, model misspecification, stale marks,
and other market effects can all be absorbed into this scalar state.

## Liquidity robustness

The extended forward panel contains **62 positive-volume holdouts over 13 valuation-date
clusters**.

| Model | MAE | RMSE |
|---|---:|---:|
| Historical-volatility PI baseline | 1.6340 | 2.3391 |
| Expanding prior-date smile | 0.1003 | 0.1358 |
| Previous-day smile | 0.1084 | 0.1333 |

For the expanding smile, the valuation-date cluster bootstrap gives:

- delta MAE = -1.5337, 95% CI [-1.9039, -1.0401]
- delta RMSE = -2.2034, 95% CI [-2.7027, -1.4213]
- bootstrap probability of improvement = 1.0 for both metrics.

Open-interest thresholds of 10, 100, and 500 preserve the same qualitative result.

The liquidity caveat is now narrower than in the original September--October experiment but has
not disappeared. Positive-volume observations are still sparse within individual 2028 expiries.
The long-maturity result is therefore strongest as evidence from available end-of-day marks and
open-interest-filtered cross sections, not as a dense transaction-price study.

## Expiry-level cluster bootstrap

For both the expanding and previous-day smile, the 95% valuation-date cluster-bootstrap interval
for delta MAE excludes zero in every expiry from 2026-09 through 2028-09. The same is true for
delta RMSE. Thus the pooled result is not generated by a single maturity.

## Interpretation

The extension materially strengthens the paper's central empirical hierarchy:

[
	ext{posterior-integration refinement}
ll
	ext{volatility/model specification}.
]

The October baseline still performs its original role: it shows that PI and posterior-mean
plug-in pricing are almost identical when the historical posterior is concentrated. The extended
forward panel then shows that changing the volatility state used by the same pricing map has a
much larger effect, across a broad maturity range and without same-day target information.

This result remains conditional on the available market marks and the one-factor pricing map.
An independent vanilla-CL volatility surface remains a valuable external validation rather than
a prerequisite for the current result.

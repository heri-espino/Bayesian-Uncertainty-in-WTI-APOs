## Scientific motivation and falsifiable question

Full original run (97,341 actual-flagged non-anchor observations; 189 dates): PI MAE 0.804044, PM 0.806818, strictly lagged AAO IV 0.218420 USD/bbl. A retrospective near-ATM diagnostic found market/current target IV mean 30.9748%, lagged IV 30.8839%, historical proxy PM sigma 26.6787%, with an 81.0% frequency of current IV above PM. PI vs PM improves price errors only ~0.0028/bbl. The historical return proxy is *changing option-parity forward averages*, not CL returns, and is not the same volatility estimand as current option prices.

**Main preregistered null:** A model receiving earlier AAO option-implied volatility information cannot improve a strong simple last-prior-date AAO IV benchmark in actual-only conditional cross-sectional MAE on genuinely future valuation-date holdouts. A failure to beat it is a valid, useful negative result. Compare error and uncertainty against *unchanged* lagged_IV on identical contracts, not old Barchart/LO data.

**Secondary question:** Within a market-IV-informed Bayesian posterior, does posterior integration (PI) yield a material (not merely statistically nonzero) improvement over its own plug-in posterior mean (PM)? Evaluate uncertainty calibration as well as point-price accuracy only if well-defined ground truth/interpretable metrics are available.

## 3. Predefine fair comparator arms

At a minimum on the same common target rows:

1. **Frozen lagged_IV** as currently implemented: last finite prior-date scalar near-ATM AAO implied sigma, max 10-day staleness. This is the incumbent to beat.
2. **Plain smoothed IV**, a simple transparent model-free baseline (e.g., rolling historical median or EWMA over previous same-expiry IV values), with length/decay chosen only on a preceding validation period.
3. **Market-informed Bayesian PM:** use strictly earlier observations of AAO implied sigma (or log sigma), with a fully specified likelihood, prior, treatment of maturity and posterior update; price at the posterior mean volatility.
4. **Market-informed Bayesian PI:** identical Bayesian fit/posterior as PM but integrate Black prices over draws from sigma's predictive posterior.
5. **Optional historical-proxy-only PI/PM**, unchanged, as a diagnostic comparator (not a fair market-information-matched method). Mark it optional to contain compute costs.

Do not pretend historical-proxy PI and lagged_IV were previously trained on equivalent information. Define Bayesian predictive sigma, innovation model and volatility positivity carefully. Consider a simple Gaussian observation model on log IV or robust Student-t errors; choose a **single primary specification ahead of final test**, document prior/likelihood/hyperparameters and sensitivity, and guard against fitting to targets. Report number of history points and posterior effective uncertainty.

Before introducing complexity, assess whether the simple median of lagged IV is a near-perfect predictor because exchange theoretical or actual-settlement marks are internally generated from a related valuation rule. This is a market-data measurement issue, not an automatic model failure.

## 5. Evaluation and inference

**Primary:** contract-row-weighted MAE on **actual-flagged common-support** settlement marks, USD/bbl, with year, maturity and side breakdowns. Also report RMSE, signed bias, sample size, IV staleness, and date-equal-weighted MAE to avoid overemphasizing high-contract-count days.

For model A vs baseline B define date-level improvement Delta_t = MAE_B(t) - MAE_A(t). Report mean Delta_t and 95% **paired calendar-month block bootstrap**, by month, with at least 1,999 predeclared resamples; dates within a month remain together. Validate with alternative coarser blocks or season/year delete-one-block if many regime changes occur. Do not treat contract rows as independent. Distinguish this corrected inference from the earlier original full run's bootstrap that mixed actual and theoretical marks.

PI-PM economic materiality should be expressed in cents per barrel and relative MAE, not a small p-value alone. If the IV-informed PI fails to improve on lagged_IV, report it candidly. If it improves, replicate by year and maturity to determine stability.

**Do not** use current target-date implied sigma as a forecast benchmark. It is a retrospective diagnostic bound, not an admissible past-information predictor. Conditional-disjoint-parity anchoring is still contemporaneous input for *all* arms; all claims must remain conditional.
